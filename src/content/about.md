I am an undergraduate at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/), studying Statistics with certificates in Computer Science and Mathematics. I have been fortunate to work with [Yang Lu](https://batmen-lab.github.io/), [Yue Zhao](https://viterbi-web.usc.edu/~yzhao010/), and [Xiyang Hu](https://xiyanghu.github.io/).

I study a neural network the way an experimentalist studies a cell. Before trusting a model, I ask whether a proposed mechanism is real or noise, whether a correct answer rests on the right evidence, and whether a result holds against a ground truth the model cannot see. I have built a test for each: false discovery rate control for circuits in language models, counterfactual controls for audio-visual models, and benchmarks that verify AI agents' work on running software and simulated physical systems. With Yang Lu, I am now bringing these tests to protein language models, where an independent experiment can confirm or refute what a model has learned.
